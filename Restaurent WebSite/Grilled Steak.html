Grilled Steak Recipe


About This Recipe

Our grilled steak is carefully prepared to reach the perfect level of doneness, using premium beef seasoned with aromatic herbs and spices. It is grilled over high heat to preserve its juicy texture and rich flavor, and served with fresh vegetables and French fries.

Ingredients

  • Beef Steak

  • Olive Oil

  • Salt

  • Black Pepper

  • Garlic

  • Butter

  • Fresh Rosemary

  • Fresh Thyme

  • French Fries

Preparation Steps

  1. Season the steak with salt and pepper.

  2. Brush it with olive oil and herbs.

  3. Grill the steak over high heat on both sides.

  4. Add butter and garlic while cooking.

  5. Cook the steak according to your preferred level of doneness.

  6. Serve hot with French fries and vegetables.

Recipe Information

Cooking Time: 30 Minutes

Difficulty: Medium
