Burger Recipe


About This Recipe

Our Burger is made with a perfectly grilled patty, fresh lettuce, juicy tomatoes, melted cheese, and our signature sauce. Served on a soft toasted bun for a delicious meal everyone will enjoy.

Ingredients

  • Burger Bun

  • Grilled Patty

  • Cheese

  • Lettuce

  • Tomato

  • Onion

  • Pickles

  • Signature Sauce

  • French Fries

Preparation Steps

  1. Grill the burger patty.

  2. Toast the burger bun.

  3. Add fresh vegetables.

  4. Place the patty and cheese.

  5. Add the signature sauce.

  6. Serve with crispy French fries.

Recipe Information

Cooking Time: 20 Minutes

Difficulty: Easy
