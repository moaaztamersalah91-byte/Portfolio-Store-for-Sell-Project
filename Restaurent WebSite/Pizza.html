Pizza Recipe


About This Recipe

Our Pizza is baked with a crispy golden crust, rich tomato sauce, melted mozzarella cheese, and fresh toppings. Every slice is full of authentic Italian flavor and made fresh to satisfy every pizza lover.

Ingredients

  • Pizza Dough

  • Tomato Sauce

  • Mozzarella Cheese

  • Pepperoni

  • Fresh Basil

  • Olive Oil

  • Oregano

  • Black Olives

  • Mushrooms

Preparation Steps

  1. Prepare the pizza dough.

  2. Spread the tomato sauce.

  3. Add mozzarella and toppings.

  4. Bake until golden and crispy.

  5. Garnish with fresh basil.

  6. Slice and serve hot.

Recipe Information

Cooking Time: 25 Minutes

Difficulty: Easy
