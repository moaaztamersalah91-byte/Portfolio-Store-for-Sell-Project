Grilled Salmon Recipe


About This Recipe

Our grilled salmon is prepared using fresh salmon fillets, lightly seasoned with herbs, lemon, and olive oil. It is grilled until perfectly cooked, giving you a tender texture and rich flavor, making it a healthy and delicious choice.

Ingredients

  • Fresh Salmon Fillet

  • Olive Oil

  • Lemon Slices

  • Salt

  • Black Pepper

  • Garlic

  • Fresh Dill

  • Butter

  • Mixed Vegetables

Preparation Steps

  1. Season the salmon with the spices.

  2. Brush it with olive oil and butter.

  3. Grill the salmon until golden on both sides.

  4. Add fresh lemon and dill.

  5. Cook until tender and easy to flake.

  6. Serve with vegetables and lemon.

Recipe Information

Cooking Time: 20 Minutes

Difficulty: Easy
