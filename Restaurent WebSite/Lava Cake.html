Lava Cake Recipe


About This Recipe

Our Lava Cake features a rich chocolate cake with a warm, gooey molten center. Baked to perfection and served with a dusting of powdered sugar, it is the perfect dessert for every chocolate lover.

Ingredients

  • Dark Chocolate

  • Butter

  • Sugar

  • Eggs

  • All-Purpose Flour

  • Cocoa Powder

  • Vanilla Extract

  • Powdered Sugar

  • Vanilla Ice Cream

Preparation Steps

  1. Melt the chocolate and butter.

  2. Mix with sugar, eggs, and vanilla.

  3. Add flour and cocoa powder.

  4. Pour into greased ramekins.

  5. Bake until the edges are set.

  6. Serve warm with ice cream.

Recipe Information

Cooking Time: 18 Minutes

Difficulty: Medium
