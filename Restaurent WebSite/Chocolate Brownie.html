Chocolate Brownie Recipe


About This Recipe

Our Chocolate Brownie is rich, soft, and full of deep chocolate flavor. It is baked to perfection with a soft center and a slightly crispy outside, making it the perfect dessert for every chocolate lover.

Ingredients

  • Dark Chocolate

  • Butter

  • Sugar

  • Eggs

  • All-Purpose Flour

  • Cocoa Powder

  • Vanilla Extract

  • Salt

  • Chocolate Chips

Preparation Steps

  1. Melt the chocolate and butter together.

  2. Add sugar, eggs, and vanilla and mix well.

  3. Add flour, cocoa powder, and salt.

  4. Add the chocolate chips and gently mix the batter.

  5. Bake until the center is soft.

  6. Let it cool slightly and serve warm.

Recipe Information

Cooking Time: 35 Minutes

Difficulty: Easy
